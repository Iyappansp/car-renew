Policy Already Lapsed?

How to renew an expired car insurance policy.

Don't panic if your policy has lapsed. RenewPath offers self-inspection tools to restore full coverage quickly.

Within 90 Days of Expiry

You are within the statutory 90-day grace period. You can retain 100% of your accrued No Claim Bonus (NCB). Record a 30-second video on your phone to complete renewal.

✓ 100% NCB discount preserved

Over 90 Days Lapsed

NCB resets to 0% as per regulatory rules, but you can still purchase fresh Comprehensive or Third-Party cover immediately without an in-person physical surveyor visit.

✓ Instant AI video approval

Avoid Driving Uninsured

Driving without valid insurance incurs a ₹2,000 to ₹4,000 fine and potential vehicle seizure under Motor Vehicles Act Sec 196. Renew online before hitting the road.

⚠ High risk of highway fines

How your No Claim Bonus (NCB) grows up to 50%.

Every consecutive claim-free year earns you a compounding discount on your Own Damage premium. NCB belongs to you as the driver — not the insurer or car.

100% Switch Portability

You never lose your accumulated NCB when switching insurance providers. Uploading last year's policy automatically transfers your discount.

NCB Protection Rider

Add an NCB Protect rider during renewal. It preserves your accrued 50% bonus even if you make 1 accidental claim during the entire policy term.

Selling or Upgrading

NCB stays with you, not the car. When selling your vehicle, obtain an NCB Retention Certificate to transfer your full discount to your new vehicle.
