Our Journey & Milestones

From a 45-day claim nightmare to India's trusted renewal platform.

How a broken claim experience turned into an industry-wide mission to fix motor insurance for good.

2021 — The Genesis

The Broken Claim & First Prototype

Following a 45-day delay for a simple bumper scratch claim, our founders quit corporate finance to build an automated policy comparison engine that exposes hidden insurer clauses.

2022 — Licensure & Growth

IRDAI Certification & 25k Policyholders

Secured direct broker licensure under IRDAI regulations. Onboarded 10 premier general insurance providers including Tata AIG, HDFC ERGO, and ICICI Lombard, protecting 25,000 drivers.

2023 — Technological Leap

Instant AI Policy Document Scanner

Launched computer vision OCR that parses existing policy PDFs or camera photos in under 3 seconds — auto-calculating exact NCB discounts and flagging dangerous under-insuring.

2024 — On-Ground Scale

14,000+ Cashless Network & Claim Desk

Expanded direct cashless tie-ups to 14,000+ verified garages across 650+ Indian towns and launched our 24/7 Rapid Claim Assistance Desk for instant roadside guidance.

2025-2026 — Present & Future

500,000+ Motorists & Digital Glovebox

Crossed over 500,000 active policies managed. Rolled out our unified Digital Glovebox featuring encrypted RC, DL, and PUC sync, alongside real-time claim progress tracking.

01

We highlight policy exclusions in bold before we talk about benefits. No misleading asterisk symbols, no disguised compulsory deductibles, and no surprises when you need.

Policyholder First, Insurer Second

When a dispute arises between a surveyor and a car owner, RenewPath always stands with the owner. We fight for every eligible rupee of repair coverage under your policy terms.

We never monetize, share, or sell your phone number to insurance call center aggregators. You will never receive unsolicited spam calls or aggressive sales pitches from us.

If an insurance concept cannot be explained in plain English to an everyday driver in 30 seconds, we redesign the explanation until it can. True mastery is making complex things effortless.
