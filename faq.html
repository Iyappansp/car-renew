Frequently asked questions.

Answers to the things people usually ask before, during and after renewal.

Renewal

Most drivers start comparing options a few weeks before their current policy expires, giving enough time to review coverage and quotes without pressure.
Driving without valid cover typically means no protection if something happens, plus potential legal consequences. Renewing on time avoids a coverage gap.
You can choose to be notified 30, 15, 7 or 1 day before your policy expires, through email, SMS or dashboard notifications.

Coverage

The legally mandated minimum coverage in most regions, protecting other people and their property if you're at fault — it does not cover your own vehicle.
Combines third-party liability with cover for damage to your own vehicle, offering a broader safety net than third-party alone.
Insured Declared Value is the maximum amount your insurer will pay if your car is stolen or damaged beyond repair — essentially its current market value.
The portion of a claim you pay yourself before your insurer covers the rest. Lower deductibles usually mean a higher premium.
