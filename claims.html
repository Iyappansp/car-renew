Protection Guide

The 4 common mistakes that void claims.

Keep these critical rules in mind during an accident to ensure your claim is approved without delay.

Trap 01

Delayed Intimation

Failing to inform the insurer within 48 to 72 hours of the incident is the #1 cause of claim investigations.

RenewPath allows 1-tap instant filing
Trap 02

Premature Repairs

Starting repairs at a local workshop before an authorized surveyor inspects and photographs the damages.

Always wait for digital inspection
Trap 03

Driving Licence Mismatch

Driving with an expired licence or by a driver not legally authorized immediately invalidates own-damage liability.

Keep digital licence updated in mParivahan
Trap 04

Commercial Usage

Using a private registered vehicle for commercial courier or commercial taxi rides without commercial endorsement.

Policy must match vehicle registration type
